# Training Data Source

## ICRISAT District-Level Climate And Yield Data

- File: `icrisat_district_climate_yield_1990_2015.xls`
- Original dataset: [ICRISAT District-Level Data: Heterogeneous Climate Effect on Crop Yield and Associated Risks to Water Security in India](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/ywp3y5j9vv/1)
- DOI: [10.17632/ywp3y5j9vv.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/ywp3y5j9vv.1)
- Attribution: Mohapatra, Souryabrata (2023), *ICRISAT District-Level Data: Heterogeneous Climate Effect on Crop Yield and Associated Risks to Water Security in India*, Mendeley Data, Version 1.
- License: CC BY 4.0. Retain this attribution and license when redistributing the source data or adapted data products.
- Coverage: an unbalanced panel of 560 Indian districts across 20 states, 1990–2015.
- Source data fields include district, state, year, production and yield for rice, pearl millet, chickpea, groundnut, and sugarcane, plus historical climate and irrigation measures.

## Important Limitations

This is historical district-level observational data, not farm-level trial data. It does not include the measured soil type, soil pH, NPK values, current market prices, or cultivation costs required by the complete crop-profit model. Do not fill those missing measurements with invented values. A separate, documented preprocessing step and additional local market/soil data are required before using it for recommendations. The raw workbook is stored here for traceable research and is not automatically treated as a trained model.
